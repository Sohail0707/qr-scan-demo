Location strategy

First it asks for GPS (high accuracy) for up to 8 seconds. Indoors, in a basement bar for example, GPS often can't get a fix. Instead of failing, it then asks for network location (Wi-Fi and cell towers), which is less precise but works inside. It shows the accuracy, so the person knows whether to add a landmark to the message.

Privacy proof

The coordinates only exist in this browser tab. They are turned into a map link and handed to WhatsApp or SMS, where the person sees the message before sending it. The security header connect-src 'self' blocks this page from sending data to any other server, and the list below is the browser's own record of every request. Tap "Copy link" and watch it: nothing new appears.
